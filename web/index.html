CareerAI

Know where your CV stands, and what to do about it.

CareerAI reads your CV, scores it the way applicant tracking systems do, builds a plan to close the gap to your target role, and matches you against live job openings.

What it does

CV analysis & ATS scoring

Upload a PDF or Word CV. It is scored across parseability, keyword match, evidence, completeness and clarity — judged against the format you actually wrote it in, whether Harvard, MIT or corporate.

Honest CV rewriting

An optimised version written to score better using only what your CV already evidences. Nothing is invented — every claim has to survive an interview.

Career roadmap

A milestone plan built from the gap between your CV and your target role, with honest effort estimates that vary by how deep each gap really is.

Job matching

Live openings for your target role, each scored by how much of what the role asks for your CV actually shows.

Where job listings appear

The job feed

1
Live listings only

Openings are fetched at the moment the user opens the tab and are never cached between sessions, so a vacancy that has been filled or withdrawn simply stops appearing. No listing is ever generated or padded.

2
Filtered to the user's target role

Only openings relevant to the role the user is actually aiming at are shown, rather than an undifferentiated feed.

3
Deduplicated across sources

The same role syndicated to several boards is shown once, keeping the fullest version of the posting.

4
Every listing links to the original posting

"Apply" opens the source site's own page for that vacancy. CareerAI does not host applications or intercept them.

5
A cover letter for the specific role

Written from the posting and the user's real CV, downloadable as PDF or Word, and grounded only in experience they actually have.

Coverage

CareerAI currently carries remote and international openings, and is adding Bangladeshi listings so that candidates in Bangladesh — the app's primary audience — can find local roles alongside remote ones rather than being sent elsewhere to look.

Platform

Mobile application for Android and iOS, built with React Native, backed by a Node.js and MongoDB service. Accounts are protected with two-factor authentication and biometric sign-in, and users can export or delete all of their data.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Who this covers

Career Assistant is a mobile application that analyses your CV, builds a learning roadmap towards a target job role, matches you to live job openings, and lets you practise interview questions. This policy describes what the app collects and what happens to it.

Contact for any question or request in this policy: rashidmostafa004@gmail.com.

Your fingerprint never leaves your phone

If you turn on fingerprint sign-in, the fingerprint itself is handled entirely by your phone's operating system. The app is never given it, cannot read it, and never transmits it. What is stored on the server is a hashed identifier for the device — enough to recognise that the phone was unlocked by its owner, and nothing more.

Who your data is shared with

It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. It is shared only with the services the app needs to function:

  • Google (Gemini) — the text of your CV and your interview answers are sent to Google's model to be scored and to generate feedback, a roadmap, and cover letters. This is necessary for those features to work.
  • Careerjet — job searches are forwarded to Careerjet's API. Their terms require the end user's IP address and browser identifier to accompany each search, so those are sent with it.
  • MongoDB Atlas — where your account and its data are stored.
  • Render — where the server runs.
  • Google (Gmail API) — used to send you verification and two-factor codes.
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose to sign in with Google, in which case Google provides your name, email address and profile picture.

Data may also be disclosed if required by law.

How long it is kept

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days, and every session requires full re-authentication after 8 weeks regardless.
  • Deleting your account schedules it for permanent removal after a 30-day grace period, during which it can be restored. All sessions are revoked immediately.

Your rights

From Profile → Security & Privacy in the app you can:

  • Export everything held about you, as JSON or CSV.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • See and revoke every device currently signed in.
  • Review your own sign-in and security history.

You can also email the address above and ask for any of these to be done for you.

Security

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt at 12 rounds.
  • All traffic between the app and the server uses HTTPS.
  • Optional two-factor authentication, by authenticator app or emailed code.
  • Accounts lock after five consecutive failed sign-in attempts.
  • Sign-in tokens are rotated on every refresh, so a captured token has a short life.

No system is perfectly secure, and this is stated plainly rather than promised away.

Children

Career Assistant is not directed at children under 16 and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe one has been, contact the address above and it will be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and the app will show a notice on next sign-in.
